FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask us

What is Abla?

Abla is an AI for restaurants that answers your WhatsApp 24/7. It answers questions, takes the full order, confirms it with the customer and prints it in your kitchen. We also offer AI for phone calls, an online store with QR ordering, a self-order kiosk and a POS.

Is it real AI or a button menu?

It's AI. Customers write the way they talk, for example «can I get two hot dogs with everything», and Abla understands.

How many customers can it handle at once?

More than 50 at the same time. Every customer gets an answer in seconds, even on a Friday night.

Do you charge commission per order?

No. You pay a fixed monthly price: COP 279,000, plus a one-time installation of COP 349,000.

What do I need in my restaurant?

A laptop and a ticket printer. We set up the rest.

Which languages does it answer in?

Spanish is included. You can add more languages for COP 34,900 a month each; more than 60 are available.

Can it take reservations?

Yes. WhatsApp reservations cost COP 34,900 more a month. If you only want reservations, without orders, it's COP 139,000 a month.

Do I need to change my till or POS?

No. Abla works separately from your till. And if you're looking for a POS, we offer that too.
