Made for restaurants that live on WhatsApp

In Colombia, a restaurant's WhatsApp never stops: deliveries, questions, reservations. Abla answers those messages for you, so you can focus on cooking.

Why Abla

Abla comes from «habla», Spanish for «talk». It's an AI that talks with your customers on WhatsApp, takes their orders and sends them to your kitchen, at any hour.

A team in Colombia

Our team visits you at your restaurant, shows you the demo with your menu and gets everything running.

No commissions

You pay a fixed monthly price. Your customers order from you, with no commission per order.
